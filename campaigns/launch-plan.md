# Campaign structure and launch checklist

## Initial structure (proposed)
1. **Search | Brand | [target geography]**
   - SketchRoot and close brand variants.
   - Brand-specific ad copy and a relevant landing page.
2. **Search | Non-brand | Dental learning | [target geography]**
   - One tightly themed ad group per validated intent.
3. **Search | Exam preparation | [target geography]**
   - Launch only if SketchRoot offers content for the specific exam terms being targeted.

Avoid combining brand and non-brand terms. Do not launch a campaign until its offer, landing page, and conversion action are verified.

## Responsive search ad checklist
- Headlines accurately describe the available product.
- Descriptions state a clear value proposition and action.
- Final URL works, uses HTTPS, and matches ad intent.
- No unsupported claims, guarantees, fabricated testimonials, or unverified credentials.
- Mobile landing page loads and signup works.
- Assets and destination comply with Google Ads policies.

## Pre-launch checklist
- [ ] Confirm account, billing, owner access, and target countries/regions.
- [ ] Confirm budget and bidding strategy.
- [ ] Confirm landing pages and current product features.
- [ ] Verify conversion tracking and attribution settings.
- [ ] Set appropriate location and language targeting.
- [ ] Review keyword match types and negative keywords.
- [ ] Preview ads and check policy eligibility.
- [ ] Record launch date and initial settings.
