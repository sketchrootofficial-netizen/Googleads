# Reporting and optimization cadence

## During the first two weeks
- Check spend, disapprovals, broken URLs, search terms, and conversion tracking frequently.
- Add negatives for clearly irrelevant queries after review.
- Avoid repeated large bid/budget changes without recording the reason.
- Verify that reported signups reflect real, usable accounts.

## Weekly review
1. Compare spend and conversions with the approved budget and goal.
2. Segment brand vs non-brand.
3. Review search terms and match-type behavior.
4. Compare landing-page and ad-group performance.
5. Check conversion quality, not just volume.
6. Record prioritized experiments.

## Reporting integrity
Use exports from Google Ads and analytics tools for real results. Record timezone, date range, attribution settings, and tracking changes. Never invent or silently estimate performance data.
